Restore a backup

Choose a backup file you downloaded from this page. It replaces everything after you close and reopen the program. Your current records are kept as a safety copy.

Restore by hand

To restore, stop the app, replace home-expense.db in the folder .home-expense-manager inside your user folder with your backup file, and start the app again. Also delete the old home-expense.db-wal and home-expense.db-shm files if they are there. Ask a family member to help if unsure.
